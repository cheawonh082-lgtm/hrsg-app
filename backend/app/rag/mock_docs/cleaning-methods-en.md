## Methods
Common gas-side cleaning methods are dry-ice blasting, high-pressure water washing and chemical cleaning. Dry-ice blasting leaves little residue and needs a short outage; water washing is cheaper but requires wastewater handling; chemical cleaning removes hard deposits but costs more and needs a longer outage.

## Selection
Select a method by weighing recoverable performance, cleaning cost, outage days and the risk of damage. Compare economics by net benefit — annual recovery minus cleaning cost and outage loss — in the calculator's method comparison table.
