## Causes
Gas-side fouling in an HRSG is the build-up of fine particulates, sulfates and rust on finned tubes. Deposition accelerates in the cold end — the economizer and condensate preheater — when flue gas is cooled below the acid dew point.

## Effect on Performance
Fouling adds thermal resistance, lowering heat recovery, and narrows the gas path, raising the HRSG gas-side pressure drop. Higher pressure drop raises gas turbine back pressure, reducing GT output and worsening heat rate. Reduced heat recovery shows up as a higher stack temperature and less steam, so steam turbine output also falls.
