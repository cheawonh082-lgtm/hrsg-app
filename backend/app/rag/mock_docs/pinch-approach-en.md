## Definitions
The pinch point is the difference between the evaporator gas outlet temperature and the drum saturation temperature. The approach temperature is the difference between the saturation temperature and the economizer water outlet temperature. Both are key HRSG design variables that set heat recovery and heating surface area.

## Meaning in Operation
If the operating pinch grows above its design value, evaporator heat transfer has degraded, commonly due to fouling. A very small approach raises the risk of steaming in the economizer.
